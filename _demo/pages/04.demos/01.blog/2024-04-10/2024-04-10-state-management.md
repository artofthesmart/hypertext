## The Crisis

### When Things Got Out of Control

I remember one particularly chaotic week where I was juggling a major client project, a personal move, and my own learning goals. My to-do list was a mess, and I was feeling completely overwhelmed.

## The System

### Finding My Flow

I decided to try a new approach to managing my tasks. I created a simple system using a notebook and a priority matrix that helped me focus on what truly mattered.

## My Workflow Rules

1. List only 3 top priorities per day
2. Block time for deep work
3. Take breaks to recharge

This experience taught me the importance of structure and balance, both in development and in life.
