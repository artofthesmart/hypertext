In an age of feature-rich email applications, I've been exploring the art of minimalism through email. My goal was to create a lightweight, text-based email client that would be efficient, reliable, and perfectly suited for low-spec systems or those who appreciate simplicity.

I chose to use mutt, a powerful and lightweight text-based email client, which runs efficiently even on older hardware. Unlike modern email applications that rely heavily on graphical interfaces and constant background processes, mutt operates with a simple, focused design.

Setting up the client was straightforward, but it required careful attention to configuration. I configured it to use IMAP for email synchronization, which allowed me to access my email from multiple devices without duplicating effort. The key was balancing functionality with minimal resource usage.

One of the most satisfying aspects of this experience was how much more intentional I became with my email use. Without the distractions of rich notifications and flashy interfaces, I found that I was more thoughtful about the content and timing of my emails. The efficiency of mutt's interface allowed me to manage my inbox quickly and effectively.

## Setup Tips

- Use text-based email clients like mutt for efficiency
- Configure IMAP for seamless synchronization
- Keep the interface simple and focused
- Reduce distractions and improve email discipline

This approach to email management made me reconsider the value of simplicity in software design.
