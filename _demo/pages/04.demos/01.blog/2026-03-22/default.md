In a world where major platforms control our information flow, I've been exploring ways to regain control over my digital consumption. My goal was to create a self-hosted RSS reader that would allow me to curate my own information sources without relying on centralized services.

I chose to use FreshRSS, an open-source RSS reader that can be hosted on my own server. This allowed me to maintain full control over my data and choose exactly what sources I wanted to follow. The process required some initial setup, but once configured, it became a reliable and efficient tool.

One of the benefits of this approach is the ability to access my feeds from any device without worrying about platform lock-in or data privacy concerns. My information sources remain under my control, and I can even modify the reader's features if needed.

Setting up the system involved installing FreshRSS on my server and configuring it to fetch feeds from various sources. I also set up automatic backups to ensure that my reading history and preferences were preserved. The process was more involved than using a traditional online RSS reader, but the benefits in terms of control and security were significant.

## Benefits of Self-Hosting

- Complete control over data and privacy
- No dependency on centralized platforms
- Customizable features and interface
- Reliable access from any device

This approach to information consumption aligns with my values of digital sovereignty and self-reliance.
