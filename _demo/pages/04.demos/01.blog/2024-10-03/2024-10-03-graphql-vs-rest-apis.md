Both have their strengths and weaknesses, and choosing between them depends on your application's specific needs. ## The First Shot

### Learning the Basics

I remember my first time holding a camera, feeling overwhelmed by all the controls. I wanted to capture a sunset, but I was clueless about exposure and composition.

## The Discovery

### Understanding Light

After many failed attempts and a few good ones, I realized that photography was not just about the camera but about understanding light and patience. Each shot required a different approach.

## Lessons Learned

- The right light can make or break a photo
- Composition is about telling a story
- Patience is a skill that improves with practice

> "Photography is a way of feeling, of touching, of loving. What you have caught on film is only a shadow of your true nature." – Richard Avedon

## Conclusion

Photography became a form of meditation for me. It taught me to slow down and appreciate the nuances in life, much like debugging code.
