Welcome to my photo gallery! Over the past **9 years**, my wife, our dog, and I have spent our weekends exploring the outdoors. What started as a simple hobby of snapping pictures on our hikes has grown into a profound passion for both *nature* and *portrait* photography.

This gallery is a curated space where I can showcase the work I'm most proud of. It’s more than just a collection of images—it’s a visual diary of our adventures and the incredible people we’ve met along the way.

### What You'll Find Here

*   **Nature Expeditions**: Sweeping landscapes, intimate macro shots of flora, and candid moments of our dog exploring the wilderness.
*   **Portraits & Collaborations**: I regularly work with friends and local artists to create striking, authentic portraits using natural lighting. 
*   **Behind the Lens**: Occasional deep dives into the gear we use, our editing process, and the stories behind the shots.

> "Photography is the story I fail to put into words." — *Destin Sparks*

Whether we're chasing the golden hour up a mountain trail or doing a relaxed portrait session in the local park, my goal is to capture the raw, unfiltered beauty of the moment. I hope these collections bring you as much joy as they brought me while shooting them. 

Take a look around, check out the collections below, and feel free to reach out if you'd ever like to collaborate on a shoot!
