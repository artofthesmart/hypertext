Private events work best when the guest list is small enough for a single conversation to travel across the table.
The team handles menu planning, timing, and the service rhythm so the host can stay in the room instead of running the room.

===

## Booking notes

1. Choose the date and rough headcount.
2. Tell the kitchen about any dietary needs early.
3. Decide whether the room should feel formal or loose.
4. Confirm the end time before the invitations go out.

<blockquote>
<p>Good events make people feel hosted, not processed.</p>
</blockquote>
