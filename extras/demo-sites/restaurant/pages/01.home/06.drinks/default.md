The drinks list is short on purpose so the staff can describe it without reciting a speech.
There is usually one spritz, one herbal non-alcoholic option, and a small wine list that changes with the menu.

===

- Juniper spritz with citrus peel
- Ginger shrub with soda and bay leaf
- Dry white for small plates
- Light red for supper

<p><small>The pairing note on the menu is there to help, not to insist.</small></p>
