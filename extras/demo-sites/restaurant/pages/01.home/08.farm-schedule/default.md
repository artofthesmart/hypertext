The kitchen works around the harvest rather than pretending the harvest works around the kitchen.
Deliveries usually arrive early, and the menu changes when a grower brings something better than expected.

===

| Day | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Valley greens | Wash and store immediately |
| Wednesday | Eggs and dairy | Reserve for the bakery and dessert team |
| Friday | Fruit and herbs | Use first, because the aroma fades fastest |

The schedule is posted so the front of house can answer ingredient questions without guessing.
