Family table is the loosest night of the month. The menu is still deliberate, but the pacing is gentler and the room makes room for noise.
The staff set it up to feel like a neighborhood dinner rather than a special occasion.

===

- Shared platters in the middle.
- High chairs by request.
- Dessert that arrives only after the last plate leaves the table.
