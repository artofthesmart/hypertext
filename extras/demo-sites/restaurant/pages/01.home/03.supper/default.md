Supper is the longer part of the menu: braises, roasted vegetables, and a grain course that changes with the season.
It is meant to be eaten slowly, with enough room between courses to notice what the kitchen is doing.

===

<time datetime="2026-10-12T19:00">7:00 p.m.</time> is usually when the room settles into its best rhythm.

## Course notes

1. Starter with acid.
2. Hearth dish with a proper sauce.
3. Salad or vegetable course to reset the table.
4. Shared dessert if the group is still talking.
