Small plates are the way the kitchen introduces itself: bright vegetables, good oil, and one or two stronger flavors to keep the room awake.
The dishes change often enough to follow the market without turning into improvisation for its own sake.

===

| Plate | Notes |
|---|---|
| Charred leeks | Lemon yogurt, herbs, and crisp crumbs |
| Marinated beans | Garlic, parsley, and smoked salt |
| Bitter greens | Anchovy vinaigrette and toasted seeds |

<details>
<summary>House rule</summary>

We will split any plate into two portions on request, but we will not pretend a shared starter can solve every argument about ordering.

</details>
