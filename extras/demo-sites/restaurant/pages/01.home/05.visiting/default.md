Juniper Table sits on a quiet corner where buses, bikes, and neighborhood foot traffic all arrive with the same amount of patience.
The room is small enough to feel deliberate and large enough to keep reservations from swallowing it.

===

## Hours

- Bakery counter: Friday–Sunday, 8–2
- Dinner service: Wednesday–Sunday, 5–10
- Kitchen break: Monday and Tuesday

<dl>
<dt>Reservations</dt><dd>Best for dinner after 5 p.m., especially on weekends.</dd>
