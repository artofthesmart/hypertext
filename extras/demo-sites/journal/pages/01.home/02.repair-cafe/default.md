The cafe smelled like coffee, machine oil, and warm dust. Someone had brought in a floor lamp with a loose switch,
and the volunteer at the long table asked for the model number before touching anything. That kind of patience is part of the service.

===

We tested the cord, tightened the housing, and found the bad contact hiding under a sleeve of brittle plastic.
The fix was simple once the symptoms were named. A smaller lesson followed: when a lamp fails in a shared room,
everyone notices the dark corner before they notice the wiring.

## Repair log

1. Unplug the lamp and label the screws.
2. Photograph the original wiring before separating anything.
3. Trim the cracked insulation and re-seat the switch.
4. Check the bulb, the driver, and the strain relief in that order.

The sign taped to the table said <abbr title="light-emitting diode">LED</abbr> bulbs are welcome,
but only if they match the fixture and do not overheat the shade.

</details>
