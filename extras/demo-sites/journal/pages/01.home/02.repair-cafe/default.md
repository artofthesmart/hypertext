Most of the failures were small: loose wires, tired switches, and parts hidden behind unnecessary fasteners.

===

The volunteers repaired twenty-two items in three hours. More importantly, owners stayed at the tables and learned how their things worked.

The evening made a strong case for products that can be opened, understood, and maintained with ordinary tools.
