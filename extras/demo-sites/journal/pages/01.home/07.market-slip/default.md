I found the slip after dinner, folded into the pocket of the coat I wore at the market. It listed pears, onions,
and a jar of honey, all of which felt more ceremonial than urgent by then.

===

## Why I keep them

- They remind me which stalls had the best produce.
- They make it easier to compare prices when the season changes.
- They mark the ordinary errands that keep a neighborhood legible.

<p><kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>P</kbd> is useful for the digital version, but the paper one still fits better beside a key ring.</p>

The honey on the slip is from the south stall, and the pears came home bruised in exactly the right way: enough to be eaten first, not enough to be wasted.
