There are seventeen public book boxes within a comfortable bicycle ride of home.

===

I spent a Saturday drawing a simple map, noting which boxes specialize in children's books, mysteries, gardening manuals, or unexpected technical references.

The finished map is deliberately printable and works without an account, an app, or a battery.
