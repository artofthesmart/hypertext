The path was almost empty, and the river carried a thin layer of mist that made the railings look softer than they are.

===

I left before sunrise with a thermos, a wool cap, and no particular agenda. By the time the city woke up,
the cottonwoods were bright and the footbridges were busy with bicycles. I stopped often, not because the route was difficult,
but because the light kept changing in small, interesting ways.

## Notes from the bank

- A pair of herons lifted from the reeds exactly as the coffee cooled.
- The old pumping station still keeps the best bench in the neighborhood.
- The sidewalks near the marina hold the smell of cedar, rain, and engine oil.

> "A useful morning does not need an agenda."
> <cite>Field note, written beside the pumping station</cite>

<details>
<summary>What was in the thermos</summary>

Black tea, an apple, and a slice of toast folded into parchment so it would survive the walk.

</details>

The river did not explain anything, which felt like a good arrangement. I arrived home with damp cuffs,
a clearer sense of the day, and a footnote for later: the mist was gone by 9:00 a.m.[^1]

[^1]: The first cold snap usually arrives in the second week of October.
