The path was almost empty, and the river carried a thin layer of mist.

===

I left before sunrise with a thermos and no particular destination. By the time the city woke up, the cottonwoods were bright and the footbridges were busy with bicycles.

The best part of the route is still the unmarked bench beside the old pumping station. It is a good place to stop, listen, and remember that a useful morning does not need an agenda.
