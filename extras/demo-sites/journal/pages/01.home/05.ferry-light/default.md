The ferry left at first light with a dozen commuters and one sleepy cyclist. From the upper deck,
the water looked brushed flat, and the bridge lights behind us were still on even though the day had started.

===

I spent most of the crossing watching the shoreline appear in pieces: cranes first, then warehouses, then the small
line of trucks waiting for the market to open. The whole trip felt less like transportation and more like a pause in the middle
of the city.

<p><small>By the time we docked, the wind had sharpened enough to turn every jacket collar into a decision.</small></p>

<blockquote>
<p>Some mornings only need a vessel long enough to get you from one habit to the next.</p>
<cite>Unpublished note to self</cite>
</blockquote>
