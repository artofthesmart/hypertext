The cart sits beside the bus stop, where the line forms naturally because nobody wants to be the person who misses the bowl.
Today the vendor had lentils, chiles, and a coriander oil that turned the broth bright at the last second.

===

## What changed at lunch

- The price board moved from pen to chalk.
- The soup got a little more pepper once the rain started.
- Three regulars traded news about the market and the bus route.

<aside>
<p>The posted price was <del>$7</del> <ins>$8</ins>, which still felt fair for something this warm.</p>
</aside>

I like meals that arrive with instructions folded into the steam: drink slowly, keep your hands close, and do not try to answer email until the bowl is empty.
