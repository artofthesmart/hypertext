I trust the weather only after the towels have seen it. Today the line held sheets, tea towels, and a shirt that always dries
a little slower than expected because the sleeves trap water.

===

<p>The forecast said <abbr title="relative humidity">RH</abbr> would fall by noon, which matched the way the cotton snapped in the breeze.</p>

<progress value="2" max="3"></progress> loads remained.

I like the line for the same reason I like a good timetable: it gives the day a shape without pretending to control it.
