I climbed the fire escape because the apartment windows were reflecting the sunset and the alley was quieter than the kitchen.
From up there, the city sounded like one long conversation heard through a screen door.

===

<details>
<summary>What the stairwell taught me</summary>

The rails need paint, the bolts need checking, and the view needs no improvement at all.

</details>

- The top landing catches the last warm air.
- The middle landing smells like rain and metal.
- The bottom landing is where people say hello when they are not in a hurry.

I came back inside when the light turned from gold to gray.
