The rain filled the curb cuts and turned the block into a rough sketch of itself. I walked the long way around the puddles,
then drew the route from memory when I got home.

===

## Route sketch

1. Start at the corner with the leaning mailbox.
2. Follow the alley until the pavement dries out.
3. Turn left at the mural and keep the bakery wall on your right.
4. Cross only after the bus passes, because the splash is worse than the wait.

> The map is never identical to the street; that is why both are useful.

<aside>
<p>By evening the mud had already changed the route again.</p>
</aside>
