Writing is where I do the part of the work that cannot be handed to a prototype.
I use it to test the logic of a product before the team commits to the shape of it.

===

- Case notes about research findings.
- Internal essays on information density.
- Drafts for talks that need a clearer argument.

<blockquote>
<p>Good product writing tells the truth about the trade-offs before the interface has to do it.</p>
</blockquote>

I prefer short essays with one clear claim, one counterargument, and enough context for another person to continue the conversation.
