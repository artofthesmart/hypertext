Some of the most useful design work happened inside internal tools: dashboards, review queues, and small utilities that removed repetitive steps.
These pages rarely earned public attention, but they changed the texture of the work every day.

===

1. Identify the repetitive task.
2. Remove the unnecessary decisions.
3. Make the remaining actions obvious.
4. Leave a trace so support can understand what happened later.

The pattern is simple, and it keeps showing up because it works.
