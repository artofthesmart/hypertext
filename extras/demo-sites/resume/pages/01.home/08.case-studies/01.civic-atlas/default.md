---
title: Civic Atlas
subtitle: Mapping local planning workflows without losing the narrative thread
---

This project combined geographic data with written context so reviewers could understand the why, not just the where.
The key design move was separating the map from the evidence trail without letting either one drift too far from the other.

===

## Results

- Reviewers found the relevant case notes faster.
- The interface reduced duplicate context requests.
- The team had a shared place for citations and assumptions.

<details>
<summary>Design principle</summary>

Keep the map visible, but make the reasoning just as easy to find.

</details>
