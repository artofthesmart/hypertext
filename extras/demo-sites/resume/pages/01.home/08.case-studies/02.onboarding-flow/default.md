---
title: Onboarding Flow
subtitle: Turning an eleven-step start into a compact, readable sequence
---

The original onboarding process asked for too much attention before the user had any confidence in the product.
I rewrote it as a short sequence with optional detail, then tested where people hesitated.

===

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| 11 screens | 4 screens |
| Duplicate explanations | One clear prompt per step |
| Hidden exit path | Easy to pause and return |

<blockquote>
<p>Users were not failing the flow; the flow was asking them to perform before they were ready.</p>
</blockquote>
