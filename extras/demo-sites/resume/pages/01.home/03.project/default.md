Civic Atlas is the project I point to when someone asks how design can make information dense without becoming hostile.
It combines data, notes, and routing tools in a system that rewards slow reading.

===

## Case study snapshot

| Area | Detail |
|---|---|
| Team size | 6 people |
| Scope | Mapping, notes, exports, and public review |
| Outcome | Faster review cycles and clearer handoffs |

I built the core layout with a consistent reading rhythm: first the map, then the context, then the decision trail.
That order kept people from missing the evidence buried in long workflows.

<blockquote>
<p>Clarity is not the absence of detail; it is the order that makes the detail usable.</p>
</blockquote>
