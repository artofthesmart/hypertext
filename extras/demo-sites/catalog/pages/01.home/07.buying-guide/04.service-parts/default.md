Any product that expects a long life should make its replacement parts visible. The spare screw, the felt pad, the driver, and the cord
are all part of the real price whether the box says so or not.

===

1. Check whether the manufacturer lists spare parts.
2. Keep a note of model numbers and finish names.
3. Save the packaging until the return window closes.
4. Replace the wear parts before the failure spreads.

That advice sounds cautious only until the first repair saves the rest of the object.
