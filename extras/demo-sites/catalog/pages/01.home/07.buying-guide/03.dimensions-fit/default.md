I keep a small ruler in the drawer because most product disappointments are measurement problems in disguise.
The trick is to compare the object against the space it will occupy, then compare that space against the way you actually move through it.

===

```text
measure width
measure depth
measure height
confirm clearance
```

<p><kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Tab</kbd> has nothing to do with furniture, but it is still my favorite way to back out of a bad purchase and check the numbers again.</p>
