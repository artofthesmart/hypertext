The first question is not whether an object is beautiful; it is whether it has enough room to breathe.
A lamp needs a reach radius, a tray needs a landing zone, and a stool needs somewhere to live when it is not in use.

===

| Measure | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Width | Prevents crowding on a shared surface |
| Height | Keeps the object from disappearing into clutter |
| Clearance | Makes cleaning and access possible |

Use painter's tape on the floor if the dimensions feel abstract. The tape is easier to move than regret.
