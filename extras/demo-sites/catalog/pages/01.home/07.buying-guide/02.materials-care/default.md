A good surface should age in a way you can live with. Steel tolerates daily handling; canvas prefers brushing out dust;
wood likes a gentle oiling schedule and a little restraint.

===

<details>
<summary>General care rules</summary>

- Dust before polishing.
- Avoid soaking seams and joints.
- Store spare parts with the product paperwork.

</details>

The right finish is not the one that stays pristine forever. It is the one that still looks reasonable after the first busy season.
