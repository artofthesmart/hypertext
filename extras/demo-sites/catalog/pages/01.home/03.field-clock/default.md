Field Clock is designed for kitchens, shared studios, and any place where a clock should be legible from across the room.
The dial favors clarity over ornament and keeps the second hand quiet.

===

## Readability notes

<time datetime="2026-10-12T07:30">7:30 a.m.</time> is the hour when the face is easiest to read: not because the light is perfect,
but because the room has not yet started arguing with itself.

<meter min="0" max="100" value="92">92%</meter> of the face is visible at a glance from three meters away.

The battery compartment opens without tools, and the movement is rated for low-noise spaces where ticking would be one annoyance too many.
