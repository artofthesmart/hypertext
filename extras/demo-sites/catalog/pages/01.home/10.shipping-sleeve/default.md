The sleeve is sized for thin objects that should not rattle around in transit.
It keeps documentation neat and makes return shipping less awkward than a loose bundle of envelopes.

===

## Notes for use

- Fold along the scored edges.
- Label the outside before sealing.
- Keep one sleeve aside as a reusable return piece.

The sleeve is mostly a promise that the second shipment will be cleaner than the first.
