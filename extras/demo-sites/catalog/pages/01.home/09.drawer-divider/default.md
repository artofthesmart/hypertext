The divider keeps utensils, cables, and desk tools from drifting into a single noisy pile.
It adjusts without hardware and grips gently enough to avoid scarring the drawer lining.

===

<details>
<summary>Setup note</summary>

Set the divider a finger-width loose, then press it into place so seasonal swelling does not make it impossible to remove later.

</details>

A good divider does not announce itself. It just turns rummaging into looking.
