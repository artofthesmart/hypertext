A compact stool for hallways, studios, and the corner where a shoe takes its time.
The joinery stays visible on purpose so the construction can do part of the talking.

===

## Joinery and finish

<dl>
<dt>Wood</dt><dd>Western red cedar, selected for straight grain and light weight</dd>
<dt>Finish</dt><dd>Hardwax oil, rubbed thin so the surface does not feel sealed off</dd>
<dt>Seat height</dt><dd>44 cm, which suits most low tables and quiet rooms</dd>
</dl>

<blockquote>
<p>Furniture gets easier to trust when the repair path is obvious.</p>
<cite>Workshop note</cite>
</blockquote>

The stool is sturdy enough for daily use and simple enough to repair without a manufacturer in the loop.
