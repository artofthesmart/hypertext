A focused desk lamp with a weighted base and a simple two-position arm.
It is built for long reading sessions, not for decorative drama.

===

## Materials and build

The frame uses powder-coated steel, the shade is spun aluminum, and the driver is a replaceable module hidden in the base.
The lamp is meant to age quietly: no glossy plastics, no unnecessary seams, and no little surprises when the bulb is due for service.

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Height | 42 cm |
| Reach | 58 cm |
| Output | 8 W LED |
| Finish | Graphite or bone |

<details>
<summary>Care note</summary>

Wipe the shade with a dry cloth and keep solvents away from the powder coat.

</details>

The soft cone of light works best over a notebook, a keyboard, or any table that deserves attention without glare.
