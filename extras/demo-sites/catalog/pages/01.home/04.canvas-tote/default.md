The tote is cut from dense canvas and stitched so the seams land where the load actually sits.
It is generous enough for a notebook and groceries, but not so wide that it becomes a black hole.

===

## Pocket logic

- Outer pocket for transit cards and keys.
- Inner sleeve for a book or tablet.
- Reinforced base so the bag stands up on a chair.

<p>The handle length was revised from <del>24 cm</del> <ins>28 cm</ins> after the first field test made shoulder carry too awkward.</p>

The tote works best when you treat it like a tool: pack it lightly, shake out the grit, and let the fabric keep the memory of useful errands.
