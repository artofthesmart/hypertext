A pair of shallow trays for paper, receipts, and the objects that drift to the edge of a desk.
The trays nest cleanly and give the surface a little breathing room.

===

## Use cases

- Incoming mail on top.
- Receipts and warranty cards in the middle.
- Spare cables or notecards below.

The edges are bent so labels stay visible even when the trays are full. That matters more than decoration when a workspace is shared.

<blockquote>
<p>Storage works best when the first tray is also the one you reach for most often.</p>
</blockquote>

<details>
<summary>What fits</summary>

A4 envelopes, folded invoices, pens, and the odd postcard that you do not want to file yet.

</details>
