This notebook is narrow enough for a jacket pocket and strong enough to survive a month of buses, cafes, and desk drawers.
The paper takes pencil well and does not complain when the page becomes a diagram.

===

## Page formats

```text
Small grid: measurements and diagrams
Blank: quick sketches and site notes
Lined: lists that should not wander
```

<abbr title="kilogram">kg</abbr> and <abbr title="centimeter">cm</abbr> are printed in the back matter so field notes can stay compact.

I use it for receipts, route names, and the little calculations that would otherwise end up on the back of a napkin.
