The refill pack keeps a drawer's worth of emergency parts in one envelope-sized box.
It is not glamorous, but it solves the sort of problem that usually steals an evening.

===

## Contents

- wood screws in mixed lengths
- felt feet for chairs and stools
- tiny cable clips
- two spare driver terminals

<aside>
<p>The idea is simple: make small repairs easy enough that they happen before the larger damage starts.</p>
</aside>
